# Creator Network AI

**A multilingual, AI-assisted content production system for YouTube and TikTok.**

Creator Network AI is a planned project to design and progressively build a repeatable workflow for researching topics, drafting original scripts, adapting content into multiple languages, preparing short- and long-form video assets, and reviewing performance across a network of five YouTube channels and five TikTok accounts.

> **Project status:** Early planning / prototype. The channel network, automations, audience, and performance targets are goals—not completed results.

## Vision

Build a responsible and scalable creator workflow that helps a small independent creator publish useful, entertaining, and original content for audiences in multiple countries.

## Initial scope

- 5 YouTube channels and 5 TikTok accounts, launched progressively.
- Short-form videos for YouTube Shorts and TikTok.
- Longer videos for YouTube.
- Initial language plan: French, English, and Spanish; additional languages may be added after quality review.
- Topic research, editorial planning, script drafting, translation/localization, production checklists, and performance analysis.
- Human review before publication.

## Proposed content pillars

The final niches will be validated through research and small experiments. Initial candidates:

1. Science, technology, and useful discoveries.
2. Fascinating history and unusual facts.
3. Geography, cultures, and the world.
4. Practical explainers and everyday knowledge.
5. Curious stories and educational entertainment.

## Proposed workflow

1. **Research:** identify a topic and verify factual claims with reliable sources.
2. **Editorial brief:** define the audience, angle, format, and intended value.
3. **Script:** draft an original hook, narrative, and ending.
4. **Localization:** translate and adapt examples, wording, and cultural references.
5. **Production:** prepare narration, visuals, captions, and editing instructions.
6. **Quality review:** check accuracy, rights, pronunciation, captions, and platform rules.
7. **Publication:** schedule or publish using platform-approved tools and permissions.
8. **Analysis:** review retention, watch time, engagement, and audience feedback; use findings to improve future content.

## Role of Claude

Claude is intended to assist with research synthesis, ideation, structured writing, multilingual adaptation, code development, documentation, and workflow orchestration. AI-generated output will be checked by a human, especially for factual claims, translations, copyright, and sensitive topics.

## Principles

- Publish original work and add meaningful editorial value.
- Verify factual statements; do not invent sources, quotes, or statistics.
- Use licensed, public-domain, or creator-owned media and keep records of permissions.
- Do not imitate another creator's scripts or distinctive style.
- Disclose synthetic or altered media when required by platform rules or law.
- Respect platform terms, rate limits, privacy, and applicable disclosure rules.
- Avoid spam, misleading thumbnails, fake engagement, and mass low-value uploads.
- Keep credentials and API keys out of the repository.
